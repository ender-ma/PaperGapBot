How to read this

  • Gap signals are leads, not proof. They come from titles, abstracts and keywords, so check full texts, synonyms and recent preprints before committing.
  • Unexplored connection: two topics that share many neighbouring topics but are almost never studied together.
  • Under-studied setting / method: a topic that is common overall but rare in your chosen context or with your chosen method, compared with what chance predicts.
  • Rising but thin / gone quiet: growth or decline relative to the whole niche.
  • Weak or unsynthesised evidence: many primary studies but no review, or mostly in silico / in vitro / cross-sectional work.
  • Novelty badge: how many indexed papers match the question's key terms together. Zero matches means none found with those words, not that none exist.
  • DEMO: synthetic papers with planted gaps, not real literature.
